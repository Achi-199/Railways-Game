Game Rules

Here are the rules of the game:

  1. Click on empty cells to place railway tracks.
  2. Each click rotates the track to fit in the desired direction.
  3. Try to connect all the tracks without leaving any loose ends.
  4. Complete the track to win, and the timer will stop.
